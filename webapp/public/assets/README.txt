BCSSL Cyber Lab — Company Logo Asset
=====================================

Place the official Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Ltd. logo here as:

    company-logo.png

Recommended: PNG with transparent (or white) background, height ~80px or more.
The application references /assets/company-logo.png in the sidebar,
dashboard, course page, and footer.

Until the image is copied here, the app shows a graceful monogram
fallback — no broken images are displayed.
